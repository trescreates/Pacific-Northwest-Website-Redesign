WHO WE ARE:

We are Pacific Northwest X-Ray, Inc! We specialize in providing state of the art x-ray equipment to medical institutions worldwide, since 1997. Our headquarters are located in beautiful Gresham, Oregon, U.S.A.

Safety is our number one priority. We ensure that all of our equipment meet federal and international safety guidelines before they are sent over to our medical companions and patients across the world.

We are one of the nation’s leading radiology and imaging supplier, thanks to the trust and respect of many of our fellow doctors, radiologists, and technicians in the field.
